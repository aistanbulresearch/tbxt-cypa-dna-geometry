# Figure legends

**Figure 1. One modeled pocket-B probe permits distinct CypA placements relative to the same displaced duplex.** Both panels show the same TBXT fragment core, transferred DNA-bound 8CDN helix (resolved residues 212–221), and displaced duplex in a shared coordinate frame, projection and scale. The closest accepted pocket-B duplex placement is 1.498 Å RMSD from the registered reference and retains 22 of 23 reference contacts. Both static conformers preserve the crystal-positioned probe heads and pass the stricter 2.6 Å probe–protein and 3.0 Å protein–protein filters. (A) In the overlapping conformer (seed 62008, draw 3069), all eight interior DNA heavy atoms within 2.5 Å of CypA (circles) lie on base pair 11 of 12 (purple). Seven are on cytosine 9 of chain C and one is on its partner, guanine 6 of chain D. The whole-duplex CypA minimum is 0.705 Å at terminal thymine 10 of chain C (diamond). (B) The clearing conformer (seed 62006, draw 1306) passes whole-duplex minima of 2.6 Å for TBXT including its helix, 2.6 Å for probe, and 3.0 Å for CypA. Labels give the nearest heavy-atom distance from each component to the whole duplex, calculated from the displayed coordinates.

**Figure 2. Interior CypA hits and crystal-like head placement in the archived MD branches.** (A) Parent trajectories and (B) continuations report post-start frame counts by replica. An interior hit requires at least three heavy atoms across at least two of the 20 interior DNA nucleotides within 2.5 Å of CypA. Each head must lie within 2 Å heavy-atom RMSD of its own crystal reference after fitting the corresponding protein. Joint counts both criteria in the same frame. Each original start has three replicas. The alternative B-on start has two. Parent runs contributed 50 frames per replica at 1 ps intervals. Continuations contributed 20 for each original start and 10 for the alternative B-on start at 0.5 ps intervals. Shading shows within-replica fractions. (C) Each dot gives the minimum heavy-atom distance from the whole overlaid 8CDN duplex to the indicated component in one continuation frame. Vertical jitter separates points. The fixed duplex was overlaid after simulation. Frames within a branch share trajectory history.

**Figure 3. The transferred DNA-bound helix narrows the pocket-C geometric results.** Counts are shown for pockets B and C with the fragment core alone and after adding the 74 resolved heavy atoms of the aligned 8CDN minor-groove helix. All evaluations within a pocket use its same closest accepted displaced duplex. (A) Admitted draws out of 65,536 torsional conformers per pocket. (B) Whole-duplex clear and (C) CypA interior-hit draws out of the admitted models under each condition. Primary probe–protein and protein–protein minima are 2.2 and 2.6 Å. Strict minima are 2.6 and 3.0 Å. These same pairs of cutoffs apply to probe–DNA and CypA–DNA. The TBXT–DNA minimum, including the helix where present, is fixed at 2.6 Å. The helix itself clears the selected duplex at 3.118 Å in B and 2.660 Å in C. Panels B and C measure separate geometric endpoints.

**Figure 4. Same-frame fit of candidate duplex placements with and without CypA.** The primary geometry gates were applied to the common panel of 8,193 rigid DNA placements for each of the 200 continuation frames, using the same frame-specific TBXT and probe coordinates in both calculations. The diagonal marks equal placement counts. Within each head category, larger markers represent more frames at the same pair of counts. A retained fit requires any accepted placement with all components. Panel loss requires a positive baseline count and a zero complete count. Baseline failure denotes a zero baseline count. Both-head markers use the same cutoff as Figure 2. Each branch contains time-correlated frames. With 32,769 proposals, baseline-positive and full-fit frame counts, respectively, were 15 and 0 for original B-on, 14 and 14 for B-near, 3 and 3 for B-far, and 2 and 1 for alternative B-on. All 10 plotted losses and all 16 retained fits persisted.

## Reproduction

Run `python make_figures.py` from this directory with NumPy, SciPy, pandas and Matplotlib. Use `--figures 1 3` to render selected figures or `--dest PATH` to direct output elsewhere. The adjacent `data` directory contains the PDB snapshots and CSV plotting inputs, with original paths in its README.
