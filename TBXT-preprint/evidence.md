# Evidence for the TBXT–CypA structural study

The central comparison concerns one pocket-B probe with its two heads positioned from binary structures. The archive contains coordinates for the conformer that intersects the penultimate base pair of a displaced 8CDN duplex and for a conformer that accommodates the whole duplex. It also contains the DNA-free snapshots and rigid DNA transformations used in the paired comparison. ## Conditional DNA integration

The fixed covering-B calculation divided the weighted near-native DNA integral by that of the same frozen probe and TBXT with CypA removed. The accepted near-native region required ≤3 Å interior sugar RMSD and ≥70% native contact recovery. In the four focused 32,768-proposal blocks, pooled retained weight was 0.207% with sterics and 0.303% with screened electrostatics. The individual block ratios spanned 0.108–0.278% and 0.210–0.396%, respectively. The corresponding ternary effective sample sizes ranged from 3.8 to 15.6 and 3.3 to 13.8. Select `poses_per_seed=32768` in the summary or `poses=32768` in the block records. ## Local scoring and independent searches

**Binary pose optimization.** Five prefiltered starts in each of four probe–protein combinations retained their cognate head within 1.06 Å in all 20 local optimizations. For TBXT, use the 5QS1 and 5QSE-to-WT rows. For CypA, use the completed 9GHY receptor table. The independently embedded B probe ended 7.24 Å from its TBXT head pose and the independently embedded C probe yielded no local pose on the original CypA preparation. The published CypA FP inhibition constants for isolated ligands 19c and 19f were 5.2 and 0.43 μM, respectively. With neutral 19c and the model-derived 19f cap on the completed CypA receptor, fixed Vina scores were −8.396 and −7.649, and local scores were −9.000 and −9.569. Lower scores favor opposite ligands in the two modes.
