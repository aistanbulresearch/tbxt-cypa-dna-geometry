# Pocket B occupancy readout

The primary quantity is the fraction of uniformly spaced production observations in which CypA overlaps the single-T-box 8CDN DNA footprint in the TBXT frame. Report it for every starting pose and velocity replica. DNA is a fixed crystal overlay and was absent during dynamics. Fractions describe the simulated time window.

## Geometric definitions fixed before examining the new trajectories

1. Remove both terminal nucleotides from each 8CDN DNA strand. Align 8CDN chain A to the source TBXT core using common Cα atoms in residues 45–208 excluding 110–121. Align each trajectory frame to the same source core. For periodic coordinates, place the intact probe at the nearest image of the pocket and then CypA at the nearest image of its probe head. A DNA heavy atom counts once if any CypA heavy atom approaches within the threshold. A frame is positive with at least three such atoms spanning at least two distinct chain-and-residue nucleotide identities. Use strict <2.5 Å for the primary result and <2.0 and <3.0 Å for sensitivity.
2. Identify the original residues contacting each head in the source pose (heavy-atom distance ≤4.5 Å). Define an engaged head as retaining ≥50% of those residues within 4.5 Å. Use the unweighted fraction of original contacting residues. Report each head, joint engagement, overlap among all frames, overlap and engagement among all frames, and overlap among jointly engaged frames. A zero conditional denominator is undefined. Report underlying head fractions and partner-aligned head RMSDs where available. Contact retention is a source-relative proxy for engagement.
3. Count sampled 0→1 and 1→0 changes of the primary footprint. For every positive and negative observed run, give its first and last frame times and observed span. Flag the initial run as left-censored and the last run as right-censored. Exit/entry times are interval-censored between adjacent saved frames. A one-frame run has zero observed span. Also report first observed entry and exit, endpoint state, and longest observed positive span.
4. Keep production separate from preparation and old observations separate from new. Do not join successive observations across replicas, or count the same boundary coordinates twice in combined windows. Report individual replicas, equal-weight replica means and observed ranges by pose, first/second half summaries and threshold sensitivity. Also calculate a descriptive trapezoidal frame-state time fraction (half weight for the two endpoints) separately from the primary frame-count fraction. Treat frames as serially correlated. Do not use a binomial interval treating frames as independent trials.
5. Compare occupancy across starting poses and blocks. Opposing seed states that persist, or repeated entries and exits confined to only a subset of poses, indicate unresolved initial-condition dependence. Crossing the cutoff within a pose does not demonstrate mixing of ternary orientations. Equilibrium population, affinity, binding kinetics and DNA competition require separate evidence.

## Branch timing and secondary diagnostics fixed before the new runs

The new branches start at the archived 50 ps endpoint of each matching replica. Exclude new time zero from discrete-frame fractions, retain it for trapezoidal integration, state-change detection and endpoint matching. Legacy production includes time zero. Split each segment into [0, midpoint] and (midpoint, end], respecting these segment-specific inclusion rules. Report each branch separately from its parent. For secondary engagement sensitivity test whether both partner-aligned heavy-atom head RMSDs are ≤2.0 Å alone and in conjunction with the 50% retention criterion. Calculate a continuous footprint boundary distance as the larger of (i) the third-smallest per-DNA-atom distance and (ii) the second-smallest per-nucleotide minimum. This value is strictly below the cutoff exactly when the footprint is positive.

## Trajectory checks

Primary branches use cumulative 10 ps trajectories (21 frames). The alternate reference-linker B-on branches use 5 ps trajectories (11 frames). Branch atom identifiers, starting coordinates and cell were checked against their parents, and each primary trajectory had to match the original 5 ps prefix exactly. The analysis used NumPy 2.x, SciPy and pandas.
