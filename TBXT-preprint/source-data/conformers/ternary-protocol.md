# Thermodynamic linkage of rigid, head-seated states

22 November 2026. Frozen before reading the joint counts. This calculation tests the excluded-volume contribution to ternary recruitment and to simultaneous occupation by single-T-box DNA. It uses the same full-probe ensembles for all occupancy states. Its state sums describe the specified model, with the structures of the binding heads held fixed. They omit affinity-producing interactions, translational binding entropy and alternate binding modes.

For each independent, ligand-only accepted torsion draw, rigidly seat TBXT and CypA in the corresponding binary head frames. Let T and P mean that each protein fits the probe separately. TD additionally requires no probe–DNA clash. TPD also requires no CypA–DNA clash. Baseline receptor–DNA contacts after superposition are measured once per target. At 300 K compute the steric contributions from normalized counts:

- Ternary factor = p(TP)/[p(T)p(P)]. The corresponding steric coupling is −RT ln(factor).
- Relative DNA accessibility in the ternary vs probe-bound binary = [p(TPD)/p(TP)] / [p(TD)/p(T)]. Its conditional steric penalty is −RT ln(relative accessibility).

These expressions follow from equal weights for accepted ligand-only draws, independent intrinsic head-binding constants, and hard exclusion. They describe already head-seated subensembles. They do not compute absolute affinity, the free concentration of CypA, protein–DNA electrostatics or probe occupancy in a titration.
