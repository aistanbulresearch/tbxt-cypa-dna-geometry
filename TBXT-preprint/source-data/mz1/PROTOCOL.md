# Full-MZ1 rescoring of the archived 2,048 VHL–BRD4 poses

Registered before computing the scores. Use the 2,048 archived rigid transforms and their model-matched 69-heavy-atom MZ1 conformers. Use only the two-chain protein template, transform/ligand arrays and original input SMILES. Do not read the experimental ternary structure, reference RMSDs, previous scores, selected SDFs or the target model ID during scoring.

Primary fixed-heavy-atom score in kcal/mol: sum the MMFF94s nonbonded interaction energies of VHL–BRD4, VHL–MZ1 and BRD4–MZ1, plus the internal MMFF94s energy of the entire pose-specific MZ1 relative to one shared isolated-ligand reference. Protein internal energies are constant for rigid monomers. Model a standard near-neutral-pH protein protonation convention. Build protein hydrogens once per monomer; optimize hydrogens on each ligand with all observed heavy positions fixed. Use a distance-dependent dielectric with coefficient 4, buffered MMFF van der Waals terms and a smooth 10–12 Å intermolecular cutoff. The common ligand zero is a locally minimized SMILES-derived conformer, not an experimental or global minimum. No parameter is fitted to the target.

Rank all finite primary scores ascending; break exact ties by original ID. Record every energy component. On the same 2,048 poses, report two diagnostic orders: protein–protein term alone, and the full score after a uniform local intraligand relaxation (0.25 Å flat-bottom positional radius and 100 kcal/mol/Å² heavy-atom restraints). The second calculation keeps both protein frames fixed, applies the same treatment to every ligand and omits restraint energy from scoring. Check stereochemistry and minimizer convergence, preserve all IDs, and report deviations. Only after freezing the full ranking join it to the previously saved benchmark labels and scores. This is a fixed-geometry energy comparison with approximate screening, not a solution free-energy estimate.
