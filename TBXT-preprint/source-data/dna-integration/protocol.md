# Conditional single-site DNA accessibility pilot

Decision record frozen before calculating the scores. The six fixed receptor assemblies are the archived B/C on, near and off source poses. Only chain A enters as target. The 8CDN chain C/D DNA is aligned with common chain-A Cα atoms in residues 45–208 excluding 110–121. Use all resolved DNA heavy atoms for sterics, and interior C1′ atoms for DNA RMSD and contact recovery. The same DNA placements, target atoms, native contact distances and quadrature weights enter every arm.

- Four independent proposal blocks with seeds 18419, 28419, 38419 and 48419. Sample 256 independent poses per crossed translation-radius shell [0, 1.5, 4, 8, 15] Å and rotation-angle shell [0, 10, 30, 60] degrees. Sample uniform volumes and restricted Haar rotations inside shells. Weight each pose by the product of its two shells' volume fractions divided by its within-shell count. This represents a local uniform volume and restricted Haar reference measure. Rotations pivot at the mean of interior C1′ atoms. The crystal pose and ±30 Å translations along each axis are deterministic controls excluded from quadrature.
- Native TBXT–DNA attraction in reduced units is −12 times the mean of exp[−(d−d0)²/8] over Cα–interior C1′ pairs within 10 Å in the aligned crystal template (d0 is their distance in the common target frame). Contact recovery uses |d−d0|≤2 Å. The TBXT, CypA and probe excluded-volume energy for each group is 0.5 k times the sum over DNA heavy atoms of max(0, c−nearest-group-heavy-atom-distance)². Primary k=2 per Å² and c=3 Å. Evaluate k=1,2 with c=2.5,3 Å. Add each group's contribution independently. These generic reduced scores have no calibrated physical energy scale.
- Primary bound-like endpoint is DNA sugar RMSD ≤3 Å and contact recovery ≥0.70. Sensitivities use RMSD ≤2 or ≤4 Å. Report local bound-like weight fraction, ratio of bound-like weighted integrals versus apo and versus fixed-probe-only, and pose counts. Absolute weighted integrals are calculated in log space and zero observed hits receive no pseudocount. Report seed spread and effective sample size. Also report geometric accessibility among endpoint poses, with no proposal bias after quadrature weighting, using the archival 2.5 Å overlap criterion for the CypA component (≥3 interior DNA heavy atoms in ≥2 nucleotides).
- Main arms: TBXT alone; TBXT + fixed probe; TBXT + free CypA; TBXT + fixed probe and pose-matched CypA. Additional component control: TBXT + pose-matched CypA with probe deleted. The free CypA model averages uniformly placed protein centers in a 100 Å cube centered on target Cα centroid with independent Haar rotations, rejecting any CypA–TBXT heavy-atom distance <2.5 Å. Four independent accepted placements per DNA proposal; free CypA has no nonspecific attraction. Include a translated-away and a DNA-centered CypA with probe deleted as negative and positive steric controls; these deliberately artificial fixed controls test the metric.
- Treat complete seeded blocks as numerical sampling sensitivity. Evaluate geometric attribution only if apo favors crystal-like poses relative to displaced controls, positive and displaced controls separate, and the main direction is reproduced across seeds. Broad seed variation, poor effective sample size or cutoff dependence limit effect magnitude. This conditional native-biased accessibility does not measure affinity, equilibrium population of a receptor assembly, a physical concentration or a binding free energy.

## Secondary screened electrostatic sensitivity

Include DNA–TBXT and DNA–CypA interactions wherever the respective protein is present. Marginalize free-CypA placement weights jointly over its steric and electrostatic energies. This changes the tested scoring model; it cannot determine ionization, explicit hydration, DNA binding affinity, or receptor assembly populations.
## Secondary numerical refinement for the B-on bound-like integral

The 3,072-pose resolution check gave a minimum residual B-on bound-integral ESS of about one. As a numerical sensitivity, use four independently scrambled 6-dimensional Sobol blocks of 32,768 poses each (seeds 7311, 7312, 7313, 7314). This evaluates only the predeclared 3 Å/70% B-on endpoint and its probe-only denominator. Because the DNA rotation pivot is the mean of the 20 interior sugars, squared sugar RMSD factorizes into squared translation plus rotational displacement. Draw a uniform rotation axis, an angle drawn from restricted Haar measure up to the axis-specific maximum consistent with 3 Å, and a uniform translation within the remaining sphere. Recover the original uniform 15 Å/60° integration measure with the corresponding exact angular and translational mass factors, and apply the unchanged 70% contact threshold. Calculate both steric and 150 mM screened models and inspect nested half-block convergence. This refinement only improves numerical coverage of the same local region; it cannot address the receptor orientation population or thermodynamics.
